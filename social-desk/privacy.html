Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Content Factory Social Desk is a personal desktop dashboard operated by the account owner. The TikTok integration is being prepared and is not a public sign-up service.

Information and purpose

When authorized by the account owner, the planned integration requests basic TikTok account identification and access to the account's public video list. It uses public video identifiers, links, publication times, and available view, like, comment, and share counts to check posts and show performance in the local dashboard.

Storage and sharing

The integration is designed to keep authentication credentials in the Mac keychain and status records on the operator's Mac. It sends authorization and video-list requests to TikTok. This website does not collect TikTok account information or use analytics; Social Desk does not sell TikTok data or share it with third parties for advertising.

Control and deletion

The account owner may revoke the app's access in TikTok's connected apps settings. Local status files and credentials can be removed from the Mac; revoking access does not delete videos on TikTok.

Questions can be sent through the project contact page.
