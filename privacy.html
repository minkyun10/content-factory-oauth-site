Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Content Factory YouTube is a personal desktop tool operated by the owner of the connected YouTube channel.

Data and use

With the account owner's permission, the tool uses YouTube Data API Services to identify the authorized channel, upload selected videos with their titles and descriptions, schedule publication and check upload and publication status. The local dashboard may show public channel video information and view counts.

Storage and sharing

The desktop tool stores its Google refresh token in the Mac keychain and stores its client configuration, publishing records and resumable upload sessions on the owner's Mac. The tool sends video files and metadata to YouTube to perform the authorized actions. This website does not collect account data or use analytics. The tool does not sell Google user data or share it with third parties for advertising.

Control and deletion

The account owner can revoke access through Google Account connections. The owner can delete local app files and the ContentFactory.YouTube.RefreshToken keychain item; revoking access does not automatically remove existing YouTube videos. YouTube content can be managed in YouTube Studio.

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google's Privacy Policy also applies to use of YouTube and Google services.

For questions, contact the operator using the project contact page.
